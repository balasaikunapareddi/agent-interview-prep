AI/ML ENGINEER — Northwind AI
(Fictional sample job description, written for this demo.)

ABOUT THE ROLE
Northwind AI builds document-intelligence products for operations teams. We are hiring an AI/ML Engineer to own the modeling and LLM features end to end: from data pipelines and model training to production inference and evaluation.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Design and ship LLM-powered features, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over large document corpora.
- Build embedding pipelines and operate vector databases for semantic search at scale.
- Fine-tune open-source LLMs for domain-specific extraction tasks and evaluate them rigorously.
- Train and evaluate machine learning models in PyTorch; run offline model evaluation and online A/B testing to decide what ships.
- Build data pipelines with Spark and Airflow to feed training and evaluation datasets.
- Own MLOps: model registry, deployment, monitoring for drift, and rollback.
- Partner with backend engineers on REST APIs that serve model predictions with strict latency budgets.

REQUIREMENTS
- 4+ years of software engineering experience, with strong Python.
- Production experience with machine learning models: training, model evaluation, and deployment.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt engineering, and RAG architectures.
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow and modern deep learning workflows.
- Experience with embeddings and vector databases (e.g. pgvector, Pinecone, FAISS).
- Solid SQL and experience with PostgreSQL.
- Experience deploying services with Docker and Kubernetes on AWS.
- Familiarity with statistics and A/B testing for model validation.
- Strong system design skills for distributed, latency-sensitive systems.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience fine-tuning LLMs (LoRA/QLoRA) for structured extraction.
- Kafka or other streaming systems for real-time features.
- Redis for caching and feature serving.
